Instructions

Press the Hollywood Quote Game button to play a game with a quote from a movie or TV show! Guess all the letters in the quote correctly to win. If you make 7 wrong guesses, you lose the game. You can also type the whole quote into the guess box to solve it outright — but a wrong solve costs you a guess.

Press the New Custom Game button to build a puzzle of your own!
